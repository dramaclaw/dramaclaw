My brave enemy 
​Shi Jun: A new morning. Usually, my mornings start the same way, but I make them interesting. I have very few friends at school; one is named Xixi and the other is Suji. They are both very important to me. And yes, I haven’t fallen in love with anyone yet. (Whispering) I am an 18-year-old 12th grader and I don’t have a boyfriend yet, even though many girls my age have already had and left boyfriends. (Shi Jun! Shi Jun!) Okay, okay, bye! I’ll tell you more later; my mom is calling me to go to school. 

​At School:

Suji: Shi Jun, you’re here! Do you know a transfer student has joined our class?

Xixi: Yes, yes! And I heard from other girls that he is very cute and smart. He is in the Principal’s office right now.

Shi Jun: Oh okay, but I’m not interested in knowing all that. (Yes, you’re all thinking right—this is the reason I don't have a boyfriend yet, because I don't get interested in boys easily) .

Nam Ji (Other girl): Anyway, you aren’t that beautiful and you fight with boys, so he won't like a girl like you. He will like me (Laughing).

Suji: Nam Ji, don't you think you're overestimating your own beauty? 

​In Class:

Teacher (Mr. Kim): Good morning, children. This is Chen Ze Yuan. (Girls screaming: Oh my god, he’s so handsome!) He is a transfer student, a basketball champion, and good at studies. Chen Ze Yuan, please introduce yourself.

Chen Ze Yuan: Hello, my name is Chen Ze Yuan. Mr. Kim has already told you about me. I hope we become good friends.

Mr. Kim: Chen Ze Yuan, go sit next to Shi Jun. Suji, you move to the first bench.

Suji: Mr. Kim, you could have asked him to sit on the first bench too.

Mr. Kim: Suji, sit on the first bench. We will talk later.

Chen Ze Yuan (Thinking to himself): Have I seen Shi Jun somewhere before? Why do I feel like I've seen her? Maybe not. (After sitting) Shi Jun, can you give me your notes for the previous syllabus? I arrived during the half-yearly exams.

Shi Jun: Yes, why not? But...

Chen Ze Yuan: But what?

Shi Jun: I need them back by tomorrow. Can you return them by then?

Chen Ze Yuan: Yes, of course. I’ll return them tomorrow. 

​Lunch Break:

Other girls: Chen Ze Yuan, come sit with us!

Chen Ze Yuan (Thinking): What nonsense. Are all the girls here crazy?

Shi Jun: Today’s food is so tasty!

Suji: No, it’s just as bad as the daily food.

Xixi (To Suji): Don’t you know? Shi Jun likes to enjoy everything (Laughing).

Shi Jun: Stop it, you guys. Let's go to class, it's about to start. 

​The Conflict and the Past

​In Classroom:

Mr. Kim: Children, you know exams are very close, so you must study harder. But before that, I will take a unit test.

Students: What? A unit test?

Mr. Kim: Yes, a unit test will help you prepare. Shi Jun, come with me quickly.

Students: Did she get into another fight? 

​In the Office:

Mr. Kim: Shi Jun, a student complained that you fought with them. Can I know why you did that?

Shi Jun: Well, sir... (nervous)

Mr. Kim: Speak up! (Angry) 

Shi Jun: He threw water on me, that's why.

Mr. Kim: You hit him that much just because he threw water?

Shi Jun: Whatever it is, sir, he started it, so now he suffers.

Mr. Kim: Shi Jun, if you do this again, I’ll have to call your parents. 

​Chen Ze Yuan enters:

Chen Ze Yuan: Mr. Kim, I had to ask you something... (Sees Shi Jun) Shi Jun, what are you doing here?

Mr. Kim: Shi Jun, haven't you left yet?

Shi Jun: Yes, sir, I’m going.

Chen Ze Yuan: What was she doing here?

Mr. Kim: She fights so much, that’s why.

Chen Ze Yuan: She doesn’t look like the type to fight.

Mr. Kim: Once she even started a fight on the road. I don't know who the boy was. But don't worry about that. Why were you here?

Chen Ze Yuan (Thinking): Could she be the one who broke my car window? I knew it was her! Looks like it's time for my revenge. I came to ask if I could get help from Jehun (the class topper) for my studies. 

​The Rivalry Begins

​After School:

Suji: Shi Jun, go home with me. If you go alone, you’ll definitely fight someone.

Chen Ze Yuan approaches them:

Chen Ze Yuan: Shi Jun, do you recognize me?

Shi Jun: Yes, you’re Chen Ze Yuan, the new student.

Chen Ze Yuan: We’ve met before this.

Shi Jun: No, I don’t think so.

Chen Ze Yuan: Yes. Some people act like they’ve forgotten everything after causing damage to others just to hide their mistake.

Shi Jun: Hello! I haven’t caused you any damage.

Chen Ze Yuan: You forgot that a month ago you broke a boy’s car window?

Shi Jun: So? What’s your relation to that? Who were you to that boy? Anyway, I don't remember his face. Do you know him?

Chen Ze Yuan: Yes, very well. Because I was that boy. Now, say sorry.

Shi Jun: No, absolutely not. I won’t say sorry to you.

Chen Ze Yuan: Fine, you won’t say sorry? You’re on my enemy list now.

Shi Jun: And you’re on mine too!

Suji: Both of you shut up! Can you even see me? Shi Jun, let's go.

Shi Jun: Fine. Chen Ze Yuan, I’ll see you! 

​Episode 2: The Prank and Results

​At home:

Shi Jun: (At night) Prepared for the unit test. Now I should sleep. Goodnight.

Morning:

Mom: Shi Jun! Get up! You have your unit test today. Your brother Fang left a long time ago.

Shi Jun: Mom, why didn't you wake me?

Mom: I told you to sleep early, but you slept at midnight. Of course you're late. 

​At School:

Chen Ze Yuan (To Shi Jun): Hey, enemy! Do you know how many mistakes are in your notes?

Shi Jun: I don't think there are any.

Chen Ze Yuan: There weren't, but I purposefully made the sums wrong in your notes. Now I'm going to tell Mr. Kim that you gave me wrong notes and messed up my sums too.

Shi Jun: Oh really? You're trying to trap me with "plus-minus" mistakes?

Chen Ze Yuan: You saw the mistakes already?

Shi Jun: Obviously. Didn't you read the notice board? I'm the math topper. I caught your mistakes in two minutes. Nice plan, but you failed.

Chen Ze Yuan: Losing once doesn't mean I'll lose every time. I'll see you next time.

Shi Jun: Of course. Let's see who has more power. 

​Results:

Mr. Kim: The results are out. As usual, Shi Jun and Jehun topped the class. Chen Ze Yuan came second. Shi Jun, you are a bright girl; it doesn't suit you to fight all the time.

Shi Jun: Yes sir, I'll keep that in mind.

Nam Ji: I only care about Chen Ze Yuan. 

​Unexpected Feelings

​In the Corridor:

Chen Ze Yuan: You came first this time, but you won't next time.

Shi Jun: Do you want to get beaten? Why do you always cross my path?

Chen Ze Yuan: Hit me if you can (Laughing). 

​The Incident:

On the way home, they see a crowd. A girl is holding a knife to her own neck.

Shi Jun: I'll handle this.

Shi Jun tries to take the knife, but the girl pulls it back, cutting Shi Jun’s hand. Shi Jun tries again, successfully takes the knife, and throws it away. Chen Ze Yuan sees this from afar.

Chen Ze Yuan: Shi Jun, what happened? Your hand is bleeding!

Shi Jun: Can't you see? Get away from me.

Chen Ze Yuan: You should go to the hospital.

Shi Jun: Who goes to the hospital for such a small injury?

Night time (Shi Jun thinking): Actually, Chen Ze Yuan isn't bad. He forgot our fight and rushed to me immediately. 

​The Next Day:

Chen Ze Yuan: Shi jun How is your hand?

Shi Jun: Better. Why are you worrying so much?

Chen Ze Yuan: (After hearing the story) Wow, you are very brave.

A girl (Jina) runs up and hugs Chen Ze Yuan:

Chen Ze Yuan: Jina! I told you not to follow me.

Shi Jun (Thinking): Who is this girl? Should I ask? No, that’s weird. I’ll ask indirectly.

Shi Jun: Oh, I thought since your girlfriend arrived, you’d stop bothering me.

Chen Ze Yuan: Girlfriend? Who?

Shi Jun: Don't act innocent. The whole class saw her hug you.

Chen Ze Yuan: Are you jealous? (Laughing) 

Shi Jun: No! (Thinking: Does he read minds? How did he know I was jealous?) 

Chen Ze Yuan: I was joking. She’s not my girlfriend; she’s my childhood friend. I think of her like a little sister. No need to be jealous.

Shi Jun (Thinking): Oh, so she's just a friend. I was jealous for no reason. 

​The Realization:

Chen Ze Yuan (Thinking to himself): I fight with her, but the truth is, I had love at first sight the moment she broke my car window. I’m just teasing her a bit.

Shi Jun (At night): Why did he call me "Ma'am"? Am I starting to like him? No, how can I? But why did I get jealous seeing him with another girl? I’m going to go crazy!
